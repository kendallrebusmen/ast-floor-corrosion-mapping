Assumptions and data-quality notes

  • StandardAPI 653 5th Edition (2014). MRT equation, section numbers, and the " Table 4.4 minimum confirmed against the owner's copy (2026-08-06).
  • 2026 depth basisROSEN Depth [%] taken as % of the " nominal plate (owner decision 2026-08-06). % ⇒ " remaining.
  • UPr basisAssumed, not measured. No underside pairs matched (2011's three underside calls were 24–36% loss — below the 2026 list's % floor). Value from ROSEN's p.332 repair-strategy worksheet at the % threshold: (" − RT) / yr installed.
  • Coating creditNone in the headline intervals. Measured topside growth 2011→2026 occurred with the post-2011 coating in place. Coated figures (StPr = ) are shown beside every scenario and in the repair-strategy table so both readings are visible.
  • Years remainingPer pit: (RT2026 − ") / (rate + UPr), capped at yr. Matched pits use their own 2011→2026 rate (clamped at 0, so UPr alone drives clamped pairs); unmatched 2026 features use the governing StPr. A pit already below the minimum shows 0.
  • Depth bandsMap colours are discrete bands of depth % (<50 / 50–69 / 70–89 / 90–99 / through-wall). The 2026 list starts at %, so its green band holds only the features at exactly 49%. Yellow and orange are low-contrast on white by design — every marker carries a hairline outline and its exact % in the tooltip.
  • ManwaysManway A (24") and Manway P (20", riveted) from the 2011 shell appurtenance table (stations 0'-0" and 183'-9", counterclockwise). Manway A's compass position (288.8°) was read from the vector geometry of DWG 5-1 and agrees with the drawn nozzle ticks within 1.5°.
  • CensoringThe 2026 PDF list contains only features ≥% depth and excludes repaired locations; 2011 recorded topside only at remaining ≤0.120". Match rate and "new" counts must be read against these floors.
  • Negative growth of pairs sized shallower in 2026 than 2011 — within combined sizing tolerance (±"). Rates clamped to 0 and flagged, never reported negative.
  • Position qualityPlate geometry (outlines, per-plate origins and axis directions) is the vendor's own Layout.MAP export from their Visio floor drawing — exact isotropic millimeters, cross-checked against ROSEN's drawn floor map (~2.5" median; the residual is page print distortion). 2011 positions verified against DWG 5-1's plotted letters (drawing precision ≈ ±20"). Eight plates carry a residual 2011 axis-polarity watchlist flag.
